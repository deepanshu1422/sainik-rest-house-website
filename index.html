Courtyard Café aur Hide-Out restaurant fresh, ghar jaisa khana serve karte hain — North Indian, snacks, aur beverages sab available hain.

Sirf 3 km ke aas-paas — auto ya cab se 10-15 minute mein pahunch sakte hain.
